FARID PORTFOLIO — PHASE 1

Includes:
- Animated name reveal and Welcome transition
- Space background, stars, nebulae, planets and meteors
- Five responsive Liquid Glass category panels
- Desktop hover and mobile tap behavior
- Submenu selection placeholder
- Reduced-motion support and Skip Intro

Run:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the FaridPortfolio_Phase1 folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html in a browser (or use Live Server).
4. Wait for intro or click Skip intro.
5. Hover a category on desktop; tap on mobile.
6. Choose a submenu item. The temporary panel marks the future gallery connection.

Next milestone: build the real horizontal work gallery with video previews, automatic movement, pause-on-hover, drag and touch swipe. Official app logos and your real work assets can be added after the gallery system is ready.
